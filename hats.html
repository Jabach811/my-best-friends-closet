Two kinds of hats

The trucker bar

Start with a hat color you like, then work through the trays of patches, pins, chains and whatever else catches your eye. It's the quicker of the two, and the easier one to talk your friends into. No two ever come out the same.

The wool western bar

A premium wool hat and a longer sit-down: bands, feathers, ribbons, charms, playing cards, branding and burning. Go western, boho, vintage or clean and modern. Tell us the picture in your head and we'll help you get there.
