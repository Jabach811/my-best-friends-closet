How it works

Call to book. Drop-offs are by appointment, so someone can sit down with you and go through your pieces properly.

Bring up to 25 pieces on hangers, plus one bag of shoes and accessories. Have more? Book another appointment.

We price what we accept. Sometimes that happens after your appointment. Your pieces stay with us for at least 90 days from pricing.

Get paid your way. We don't call each time something sells, so call anytime to check your balance. Once it reaches $20, ask for a check. Checks go out on the 15th. Or spend your balance in the shop.

What we take, and what we don't

Yes, please

  • Current-season pieces
  • On hangers, washed or dry-cleaned
  • Excellent condition, no wear
  • Curated and designer labels
  • Shoes, bags and jewelry

Not this time

  • Fast fashion
  • Stains, pilling or missing buttons
  • Off-season pieces
  • Smoke odor, pet hair or counterfeit items
  • More than 25 pieces at once

The fine print

This sums up our consignment policy. Your signed agreement is what counts.

  • Items stay with us for at least 90 days from pricing, which may happen after drop-off. After 90 days, pieces that are still in season or in demand may stay out longer.
  • Your share is 40% of the sale price, before tax. Buyers pay a 99¢ handling fee on items over $10.
  • After 30 days, prices may drop 15–50% depending on demand and season. Jewelry, luxury pieces and anything with a written exception may be excluded. Overstock and out-of-season pieces may be cleared or repriced.
  • Call to ask for a check. You need a balance of at least $20. Checks are issued on the 15th, and mailing one costs $2. You can use store credit instead.
  • Pieces we decide won't sell are donated, unless your agreement says not to. There's a $25 fee to find items for pickup.
  • If we call about pieces we didn't accept, pick them up within 7 days or they'll be donated.
  • Money not collected within 12 months of a check request is forfeited. After 12 months with no contact, unpaid commission and remaining items become the shop's.
