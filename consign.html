Closet feeling full?

Turn what you loved once into a check.

Bring us the pieces that no longer get worn. We price them, put them on the floor, and send you 40% of everything that sells. No photographs to take, no shipping labels, no strangers at your door.

How it works

Call the shop and we'll set a time. Appointments matter here — it means someone can sit down with you and go through the pieces properly instead of squeezing it between customers.

We look at everything together, agree on prices, and your pieces go on the floor for ninety days. Checks go out on the 15th of every month. Whatever hasn't sold by day ninety, you can pick up, or we'll donate it locally — your call, and we'll ask before we do anything.

The short version

  • Up to 25 pieces per visit
  • You earn 40% of the sale price
  • Paid by check on the 15th
  • Ninety days on the floor
  • Prices agreed with you, not to you
  • Unsold pieces back or donated

Before you pack the bag

What we take, and what we don't

Yes, please

  • Current-season pieces
  • Freshly laundered and pressed
  • Excellent condition, no wear
  • Curated and designer labels
  • Shoes, bags and jewelry

Not this time

  • Fast fashion
  • Stains, pilling or missing buttons
  • Off-season pieces
  • Anything that smells of smoke
  • More than 25 pieces at once

Not sure?

Bring it anyway. It's easier for both of us to look at the piece than to describe it over the phone, and we'd rather say yes to something you were unsure about than miss it.

Asked at the counter every week

Questions we get

Do I need an appointment?
Yes. Call and we'll find you a slot. It's the only way to give your pieces the time they deserve.
Who decides the price?
We do it together, at the appointment. We price for what actually sells in Tracy, and we'd rather explain the number than surprise you with it.
When do I get paid?
Checks go out on the 15th of the month for anything that sold the month before.
What if nothing sells?
After ninety days you can collect your pieces, or ask us to donate them locally. We won't donate anything without checking with you first.
Can I bring more than 25 pieces?
Not in one visit — 25 is what we can properly review and get on the floor. Come back with the next batch whenever you like.
