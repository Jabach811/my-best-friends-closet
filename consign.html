How it works

Call the shop and we'll set a time. Appointments matter here — it means someone can sit down with you and go through the pieces properly instead of squeezing it between customers.

Items are reviewed and priced after drop-off; this may not happen during your appointment. The minimum ninety-day holding period begins when items are priced. Payout checks are issued on the 15th after you request one, with a $20 minimum balance. You can also use your balance as store credit.

Before you pack the bag

What we take, and what we don't

Yes, please

  • Current-season pieces
  • On hangers, washed or dry-cleaned
  • Excellent condition, no wear
  • Curated and designer labels
  • Shoes, bags and jewelry

Not this time

  • Fast fashion
  • Stains, pilling or missing buttons
  • Off-season pieces
  • Smoke odor, pet hair or counterfeit items
  • More than 25 pieces at once

Not sure?

Call before your appointment if you are unsure about a brand, season or condition. We can help you decide what to bring.

Asked at the counter every week

Questions we get

Do I need an appointment?
Yes. Call and we'll find you a slot. It's the only way to give your pieces the time they deserve.
Who decides the price?
The shop reviews and prices accepted items. Pricing may happen after drop-off. Written exceptions can apply to particular items.
When do I get paid?
Call to check your balance and request a payout. Checks are issued on the 15th with a $20 minimum balance; mailing a check costs $2. Store credit is also available. The shop does not call when individual items sell.
What if nothing sells?
After ninety days, items may remain if still seasonal or in demand. Items the shop determines will not sell are donated unless your contract says not to donate. A $25 locating fee applies to pickup. Contact the shop to arrange it.
Can I bring more than 25 pieces?
The limit is 25 hung items per appointment. You may also bring a bag of shoes and accessories. Call to arrange another appointment for additional clothing.

Before you consign

These details summarize the shop's published policy. Your signed consignment agreement governs your items.

  • Items stay with the shop for at least 90 days from pricing, which may happen after drop-off. A $25 fee applies to locating items for pickup.
  • Your share is 40% of the sale price excluding tax. Buyers pay a 99-cent handling fee on items priced over $10.
  • After 30 days, prices may be reduced 15–50% according to demand and season. Jewelry, luxury pieces and written exceptions may be excluded. Overstock and out-of-season pieces may be cleared or repriced.
  • Request payouts by calling. A check needs at least a $20 balance, is issued on the 15th, and can be mailed for $2. You may use store credit instead.
  • Money uncollected for 12 months after a check request is forfeited. After 12 months without contact, unpaid commission and remaining items become shop property.
  • Donation of unsold items follows your contract. If the shop calls about declined items, collect them within seven days or they will be donated.
