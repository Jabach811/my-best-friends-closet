Before you shop

Store policies

A quick guide to returns and shopping with us. The store's full published policies and your signed consignment agreement govern where applicable.

Returns & store credit

Request a return or exchange within 14 days of delivery. Approved returns receive store credit. Keep items unused and unworn, with tags, original packaging and proof of purchase.

Email bestfriendscloset@att.net before sending anything back. Returns sent without approval are not accepted. Ask the shop about any item-specific questions before purchasing.

Consignment

Appointments, payout requests, markdowns, pickup fees and donation conditions apply. Read the consignment guide.

Shopping terms

Shopify handles checkout, payment and order confirmation. Prices, available quantities, shipping and pickup options are confirmed there. Adding an item to this site's bag does not reserve it or create an order.

The store's terms cover orders, pricing, product information, acceptable use, third-party services and limitations. Read the full store terms ↗

Privacy & your information

Your bag is saved in this browser. This site does not collect payment details. Shopify receives the items you choose when you continue to checkout. The email form sends your address directly to the store's Shopify subscription form for confirmation.

For the store's handling of orders, customer information and privacy requests, view its published privacy policy ↗ or email bestfriendscloset@att.net.
