Store policies

A quick guide to returns and shopping with us. Our full policies, and your signed agreement if you consign, are what count.

Returns & store credit

You can return or exchange an item within 14 days of delivery. Approved returns get store credit. Items need to be unworn, with tags, original packaging and proof of purchase.

Email us at bestfriendscloset@att.net before sending anything back. We can't accept returns we haven't approved. Questions about a piece? Ask us before you buy.

Consignment

Drop-offs are by appointment. Payouts, markdowns, pickup fees and donations follow your signed agreement. Read the fine print.

Shopping online

Checkout, payment and order confirmation happen on our Shopify store. That's where prices, stock, shipping and pickup are confirmed. Adding a piece to your bag doesn't hold it for you.

Our store terms cover orders, pricing, product details and the rest. Read our full terms ↗

Privacy & your information

Your bag is saved in your own browser. You enter card details only at Shopify checkout, never on this website. When you join our email list, your address goes straight to our Shopify store, which emails you to confirm.

To see how we handle orders and customer information, read our privacy policy ↗ or email bestfriendscloset@att.net.
