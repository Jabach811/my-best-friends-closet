How it works

You pick a chain — premium, gold-filled, sterling silver, or a bit of colored enamel. We measure it against your wrist, ankle, or hand, then weld the two ends together right there on the spot. The whole thing takes about ten minutes, and no, it doesn't hurt.

People come in for birthdays, graduations, bridal showers, mother-and-daughter afternoons, or no reason at all. It ends up being less about the jewelry and more about who you were with when you got it.

Before you come in

Questions we get asked

Does it hurt?
Not at all. The weld happens on a small tab away from your skin — most people say they don't feel a thing.
Can I take it off?
Yes. Snip it at the jump ring with a pair of scissors, keep the chain, and bring it back whenever you want it rewelded.
What about airport security?
No trouble. It's fine through TSA and fine to wear every day.
How long does it last?
Gold-filled and sterling silver hold up for years with normal care.
Do I need an appointment?
Walk-ins are welcome when we have room. If you're bringing a group or celebrating something, call first and we'll book the time.
