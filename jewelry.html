How it works

Pick a chain: premium, gold-filled, sterling silver or colored enamel. We measure it on your wrist, ankle or hand, then weld the ends together right there. It's quick, and no, it doesn't hurt.

People come in for birthdays, graduations, bridal showers, mother-and-daughter afternoons, or no reason at all. It ends up being less about the jewelry and more about who you were with when you got it.

Questions we get asked

Does it hurt?
Not at all. It's quick and painless.
Can I take it off?
Yes. Snip it with scissors and keep the chain. Bring it back when you want it welded on again.
What about airport security?
No trouble. It's fine through TSA and fine to wear every day.
How long does it last?
It's made for everyday wear. If it ever breaks, bring it in. Repairs are $10.
