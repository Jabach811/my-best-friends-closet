Consignment, made easy

Bring us the pieces you loved once — we'll find them their next best friend. You earn 40% of every sale, paid monthly. No spreadsheets, no shipping labels, no strangers at your door. Call (209) 833-6232 and we'll set a time.

  1. 01

    Gather your best

    Up to 25 freshly-laundered, on-trend pieces in excellent condition. Current season, curated brands — no fast fashion.
