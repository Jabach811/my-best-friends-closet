Consignment, made easy

Make room for what's next. You earn 40% of the sale price, before tax. Call (209) 833-6232 to book a drop-off. Once your balance reaches $20, request a check, or spend it in the shop anytime.

  1. 01

    Gather your best

    Up to 25 pieces on hangers, plus one bag of shoes and accessories. Freshly laundered, in excellent condition, in style now. No fast fashion.

  2. 02

    Drop them off

    Drop-offs are by appointment. We price what we accept, and it stays with us for at least 90 days.

  3. 03

    Request your payout

    Call to request a check. Checks go out on the 15th once you have $20 or more, or use your balance as store credit anytime.

Meet the owner

My Best Friend's Closet started the way the name says: friends trading clothes. Liz Garcia filled the first racks from her best friends' closets, and the shop moved into 53 W 10th Street in 2015.

Today it's about half new boutique styles and half consignment, and Liz still runs the floor.
