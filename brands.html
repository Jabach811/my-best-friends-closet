What we have to pass on

No thank you

  • Fast fashion, like Shein, Temu, Forever 21 and H&M
  • Fakes of any kind
  • Stains, holes, pilling or signs of wear
  • Smoke odor or pet hair
  • Off-season pieces

Why we say no

It isn't snobbery. It's what sells. A piece that sits on the rack for 90 days earns you nothing. We'd rather turn down five things and sell the other twenty.

Not sure?

Bring it to your appointment anyway. It's easier to look at a piece together than to describe it over the phone. Full terms are on the consign page.

Questions about brands

Do you take designer handbags?
Yes. Bring the bag to your appointment and we'll look at it with you. We don't take fakes of any kind.
My brand isn't on the list. Is that a no?
No. The list is a guide, not a rulebook. Condition and season matter more than the tag inside the collar.
How do I know if something is new or consigned?
Online, designer resale pieces are marked on the photo and on their page. Everything else online is new. In the shop, just ask and we'll tell you.
