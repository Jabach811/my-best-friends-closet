Before you pack the bag

What we have to pass on

No thank you

  • Shein, Temu and fast fashion
  • Forever 21, H&M basics
  • Old Navy and mall-brand basics
  • Anything worn, pilled or stained
  • Off-season pieces
  • Anything that smells of smoke

Why we say no

It isn't snobbery — it's what sells. A piece that sits on the rack for ninety days earns you nothing and takes up space that could have earned you something. We'd rather turn down five things and sell the other twenty.

Not sure?

Bring it anyway. It's easier for both of us to look at the piece than to describe it over the phone, and we'd rather say yes to something you were unsure about than miss it. Full terms are on the consign page.

Asked at the counter every week

Questions about brands

Do you take designer handbags?
Yes, and they're one of the fastest things we sell. Bring the bag in and we'll look at it with you. If it's a luxury label we authenticate it before it goes on the floor.
My brand isn't on the list. Is that a no?
No. The list is the labels we see most, not a rulebook. Condition and season matter more than the tag inside the collar.
How do I know if something is new or consigned?
Every piece on the website carries a tag — New or Consigned — on the photo and again on the item's page. In the shop, just ask; we'll tell you straight.
Do you take kids' clothes?
We carry newborn through size 8 in the shop, next to the fitting rooms. Bring children's pieces to your appointment and we'll go through them with everything else.
